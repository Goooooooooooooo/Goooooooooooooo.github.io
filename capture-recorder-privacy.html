本地数据处理

Capture Recorder 是截图、录屏、编辑与导出工具。扩展会处理您选择的内容，其中可能包含个人信息，不能理解为“不处理个人数据”。扩展不向开发者上传截图、录像或录音，不提供账户、云同步、广告、分析或追踪服务。

仅在您主动操作并获得必要的浏览器/系统许可后，扩展访问选定网页、屏幕、窗口、摄像头、麦克风或来源音频。它也处理主动导入/粘贴的图片、编辑内容、名称、时间、尺寸及来源标签页标识/URL 等必要任务元数据，不持续收集浏览历史。

存储、删除和安全

作品、草稿、缩略图、录制分片和任务记录保存在扩展的 IndexedDB；偏好保存在 chrome.storage.local。待下载的转换副本可能暂存在页面内存。作品保留到手动删除、扩展数据被清除或扩展被卸载。中断分片可能为抢救而保留，抢救无法继续原录制；完成录制后会尝试清理临时分片，清理失败可能残留。任务等运行记录可能保留到扩展数据被清除。存储提醒不会自动删除作品。

下载文件是独立副本：下载不会删除浏览器作品，删除作品不会删除下载文件。请自行备份重要内容并管理下载目录。扩展没有自行实施媒体加密，不是加密保险箱。设备安全和浏览器存储可用性仍会影响数据安全。

权限与外部交互

  • activeTab：用户触发的当前网页访问和截图。
  • scripting：选区及全页测量、滚动和临时样式恢复。
  • storage：保存偏好。
  • tabCapture：用户触发的标签页录制。

摄像头、麦克风和屏幕共享按浏览器/系统授权处理；录制控制页必须保持打开。无全站内容访问权限或后台上传。点击“联系作者”交由邮件应用处理；您自行发送的邮件及内容由作者与邮件服务商处理，用于回复支持或隐私问题，并按解决问题的需要保留。可邮件请求删除，请勿发送不必要的敏感内容。不会自动附加或发送作品。

通过 Chrome API 获得的数据仅用于声明的用户功能，遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策及 Limited Use 要求，不出售、不用于广告或信用评估。开源组件许可随扩展附带。重要政策变更将在本页和版本说明披露。

Local data handling

Capture Recorder handles user-selected media and content that may include personal information. After user action and applicable permission, it accesses the selected page, screen, window, camera, microphone or source audio, imported/pasted images, edits and necessary names, timestamps, dimensions and source tab identifiers/URLs. It does not continuously collect browsing history. It does not upload captures to the developer and has no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics or tracking service.

Storage and deletion

Media, drafts, thumbnails, chunks and task records use extension-local IndexedDB; preferences use chrome.storage.local. Conversion copies may remain in page memory. Items remain until manually deleted or extension data is cleared/uninstalled. Interrupted chunks may remain for recovery, which cannot resume recording. Temporary chunks are cleaned after successful recording where possible; failures may leave remnants. Task records may remain until extension data is cleared. Storage reminders do not delete items.

Downloads are independent copies. Downloading does not remove browser copies; deleting browser copies does not remove downloads. Back up important content. The extension does not implement its own media encryption and is not an encrypted vault.

Permissions and contact

activeTab enables user-triggered page access; scripting enables selection/full-page capture; storage saves preferences; tabCapture records the selected tab. Camera, microphone and screen sharing need applicable browser/system permission. Keep the recording control page open.

Contact links open your email application. Emails you choose to send are handled by the author and email provider to respond to your request and retained as needed to resolve it; contact the author to request deletion. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information. Captures are never automatically attached or sent.

Chrome API data is used only for declared user-facing functions under Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements, not sold or used for advertising or credit assessment.
